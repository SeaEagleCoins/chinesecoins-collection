Ancient Chinese coins: A brief history of 5 Dynasties coins

The 5 Dynasties and 10 Kingdoms period was a turbulent period characterised by frquent regime changes and intense rivaries among warring factions.

Most of the coins issued during this period were crudely made and were of inferior quality. very diffrent from the highly standardized and high quality coins from the preceeding Tang Dynasty. Generally coins from this era are quite scarce and are well sought after by collectors.
