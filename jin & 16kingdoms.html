Ancient Chinese coins: A brief history of coins from the Jin Dynasty & 16 Kingdoms

The Jin Dynasty, which lasted from AD265 to 420, was established following the end of the 3 Kingdoms period. Once again, China was unified for the next few decades.

However conflicts among rival groups resulted in severe political fragmentation and it eventually led to the formation of 16 Kingdoms; Cheng Han, Former Liang, and Later Zhao etc.

Notable coins from this period were "Yan Huan Wu Zhu" and "Zao Bian Wu Zhu". These were actually 1 single "Wu Zhu" coin. The outer rim of the coin was cut away to form the "Yan Huan Wu Zhu" and the remaining central piece containing the square hole of the coin became "Zao Bian Wu Zhu".

The "Han Xing" coin issued by Emperor Li Shou of the Chen Han Kingdom, one the 16 Kingdom, was the 1st coin to adopt the emperor's reign tile on the coin.
