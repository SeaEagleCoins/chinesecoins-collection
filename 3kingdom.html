Ancient Chinese coins: A brief history of 6 coins from 3 Kingdoms

Three rival states; Cao Wei led by Cao Cao, Shu Han led by Liu Bei, and Eastern Wu led by Shun Quan, emerged following the collapse of the Han Dynasty in AD 220. It was a period of political chaos and constant warfares among these 3 states.

Due to scarcity of metal, large denomination cash coins were issued to finance the wars.

The State of Cao Wei issued mainly the relative more standard and stable "Wu Zhu coins.

Su Han issued issued mainly "Zhi Bai Wu Zhu", a relatively heavy and thick coin.

Eastern Wu issued a variety of large denomination coins; "Da Quan Wu Bai" - (Large Currency of 500 cash) and "Da Quan Dang Qian" - (Large Currency of 1000 cash),
